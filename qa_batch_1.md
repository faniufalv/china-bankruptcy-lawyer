## Q1: Under the latest law, What are the new regulations on registered capital under the new Company Law?

**A**: The 2024 revised Company Law: 1) LLC shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within 5 years from establishment; 2) Joint-stock company promoters must pay upon establishment; 3) State Council may set special provisions. Companies should evaluate capital payment plans promptly.

**法律依据**: 《最高人民法院关于适用〈公司法〉若干问题的规定》

**领域**: 新公司法 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Financial Compliance] What documents do foreigners need to work in China?

**A**: Foreigners working in China need a work permit and work-type residence permit. Process: 1) Employer applies for Foreigner Work Permit Notice; 2) Foreigner applies for Z visa at Chinese embassy; 3) Within 30 days of entry, obtain Work Permit; 4) Apply for residence permit.

**法律依据**: 《刑事诉讼法》

**领域**: 刑事辩护 | **语言**: English
